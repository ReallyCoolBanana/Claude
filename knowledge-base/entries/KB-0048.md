# Session 3: SEC EDGAR Integration and Sector Deep Dive

## Context
TEAM-0037 executed Session 3 of the 5-session roadmap: building the SEC EDGAR filing retrieval tool, conducting sector deep dives, and producing 3 new picks across Utilities, Gold Mining, and Defense — sectors with strong macro catalysts (AI power demand, gold at $5,300+, Iran conflict/defense spending).

## Method
### Infrastructure (20%)
- Built `method_sec_edgar.py` — CLI tool for SEC EDGAR filing data retrieval
  - Looks up CIK from ticker via SEC's company_tickers.json
  - Fetches filings from data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK{cik}.json
  - Filters by filing type (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K) with configurable limit
  - Table and JSON output formats
  - Rate-limited for SEC compliance
  - Tested successfully with AAPL and MSFT

## Lessons Learned
1. **Macro catalyst alignment produces highest-conviction picks.** VST (AI power demand), NEM (gold price surge), and GD (defense spending) all have clear, identifiable catalysts vs. generic "undervalued" theses.
2. **3 quality picks > 5 mediocre ones.** Session 3 produced fewer picks than Sessions 1-2 but with stronger catalyst alignment and higher average confidence.
3. **SEC EDGAR is valuable for verification, not screening.** Filing data confirms fundamentals but doesn't replace WebSearch for real-time sentiment and analyst consensus.
4. **Portfolio still lacks hedges.** All 12 picks are long. VIX 25+ environment warrants at least one defensive/inverse position.
