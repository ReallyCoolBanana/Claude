## Lessons Learned
1. **Same-day portfolio reviews show limited price movement.** All picks were entered and reviewed on March 12. Real performance data will emerge over weeks.
2. **Thesis strengthening is as important as price movement.** 4 of 12 theses strengthened based on new catalysts (not just price), indicating good initial selection.
3. **NVO was correctly flagged as highest risk.** The FDA warning that was a risk factor at entry has now materialized. The medium confidence rating was appropriate.
4. **Script indexing discipline is critical.** Sessions 1-3 created 13 new scripts without indexing them, breaking tool discoverability. Future sessions must index scripts at creation time.
5. **Source file naming mismatch is systemic.** 42 source files have descriptive suffixes that don't match the index. This predates our sessions and requires a dedicated remediation effort.

## Recommendations
