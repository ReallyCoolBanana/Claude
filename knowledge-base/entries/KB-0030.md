# Cross-Team Analysis: 28 Sessions, 132 Agents

## Summary

Comprehensive analysis of all 28 team sessions, 29 KB entries, and 13 SOPs. Produced structured analysis archive, 2 SOP updates, 3 new SOPs, and team improvement procedures.

## Key Findings

1. **Solo agents are most efficient** (4.0 artifacts/agent) while teams of 20+ lose 89% efficiency
2. **Optimal team size is 5-8** for integrated operations (T27: full audit in 90s with 5 agents)
3. **7 consecutive inward-facing teams** (T22-T28) — critical recursion risk
4. **SOP-018 (Market Research) never executed** — zero picks logged
5. **13 SOPs approaching saturation** — need lifecycle management
6. **2 high-severity bugs unfixed** — data corruption and authorization risks
7. **Bug detection matured** from 0.06 bugs/agent (ad hoc) to 0.93 bugs/agent (systematic)
