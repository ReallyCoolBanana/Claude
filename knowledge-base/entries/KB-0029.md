# Best Practices Sorting Schema and Archive

## Context
SORT-ARCHIVE-3 was tasked with creating a structured, actionable archive of software engineering best practices sourced from DATA-GATHER-PRACTICES. A sorting schema was needed to classify practices by category, priority, and implementation difficulty so teams can prioritize adoption.

## Method
Designed a 3-axis classification system:
1. **Category** (7 values): Design Patterns & Architecture, Testing & QA, Security, Performance Engineering, CI/CD & DevOps, Code Review & Collaboration, Documentation Standards.
2. **Priority** (4 levels): Critical > High > Medium > Low. Critical = failure to adopt creates significant risk.
3. **Difficulty** (3 levels): Easy (days) > Medium (weeks) > Hard (months+).

Each practice entry includes: name, category, priority, difficulty, rationale, implementation steps, enforcement tools, and source URLs.

Populated the archive with 18 well-established industry practices across all 7 categories while awaiting upstream DATA-GATHER-PRACTICES findings.

## Result
- 18 practices archived across 7 categories.
- 5 Critical priority, 11 High, 2 Medium.
- 6 "quick wins" identified (High priority + Easy difficulty): Secrets Management, Dependency Vulnerability Scanning, Reproducible Builds, Small PRs, Automated Linting, ADRs.
- Archive written to `storage/coordination/teams/sorted_archive_3.md`.
- Bus message sent to think tank on `sa-3-to-thinktank` channel.

## Lessons Learned
- The 3-axis schema (category/priority/difficulty) is effective for making practices actionable. Teams can filter by "Critical + Easy" to find urgent quick wins.
- A "Quick Wins" section in the summary matrix helps leadership identify low-hanging fruit.
- Populating the schema with known practices before upstream data arrives means the archive is immediately useful rather than empty.

## Recommendations
- Future teams should extend this archive as DATA-GATHER-PRACTICES delivers findings.
- Consider adding a "maturity" axis (Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced) for teams at different stages.
- Track adoption status per practice per team to measure progress.
