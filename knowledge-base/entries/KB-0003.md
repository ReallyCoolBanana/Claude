# Multi-Team Competitive Research: Quantitative Stock Analysis with AI

## Context
Tested the Sonnet Research API and multi-agent coordination by running a 5-team competitive research operation on quantitative stock analysis techniques with AI.

## Method
- **Team 1** (lead + 8 sub-agents): Broad deep research across 8 areas
- **Team 2** (lead + 4 sub-agents): Focused deep research on 4 areas
- **Team 3** (lead + 3 sub-agents): Verification and grading of Team 1
- **Team 4** (lead + 3 sub-agents): Verification and grading of Team 2 + head-to-head
- **Team 5** (lead + 4 sub-agents): Coding infrastructure (grading scripts, merger tools, category schemas)

Teams ran in parallel phases: Phase 1 (Teams 1, 2, 5 concurrently), Phase 2 (Teams 3, 4 concurrently after Phase 1).

## Result

### Research Coverage
- **8 categories**: ML trading, NLP/sentiment, RL portfolio optimization, time series forecasting, alternative data, HFT, risk management, XAI/governance
- **221 unique techniques** identified across both teams
- **72 sources** cited total (40 from Team 1, 32 from Team 2)
- **23 missing citations** found by verification teams
- **5 inaccuracies** identified and corrected

### Grades
| Team | Automated | Manual | Letter |
|------|-----------|--------|--------|
| Team 1 | 84.38/100 | 80/100 | B+ |
| Team 2 | 78.12/100 | 81/100 | B+ |

### Winner: Team 1 (by narrow margin)
- Team 1 won on breadth (8 vs 4 areas) and citation diversity
- Team 2 won on depth and practical implementation detail
- Both scored B+ overall

### Top Techniques Identified
1. Gradient boosting (XGBoost/LightGBM) for alpha factor generation
2. Temporal Fusion Transformers for time series forecasting
3. Deep Reinforcement Learning (PPO/SAC) for portfolio optimization
4. FinBERT/FinGPT for financial sentiment analysis
5. Graph Neural Networks for market relationship modeling

## Lessons Learned
1. **More sub-agents != proportionally better** — Team 1 (8 agents) only marginally beat Team 2 (4 agents) despite 2x the workforce. Quality research requires depth, not just breadth.
2. **Verification teams are essential** — Teams 3 and 4 found 23 missing citations and 5 inaccuracies. Unchecked AI research has citation quality issues.
3. **Parallel team deployment works well** — Running non-dependent teams concurrently maximized throughput.
4. **Infrastructure team (Team 5) should deploy first** — Having grading tools and category schemas ready before verification teams start is critical.
5. **File I/O from sub-agents can fail** — Sub-agents may not always persist files; capturing data in return transcripts provides a backup.

## Recommendations
1. Always pair research teams with verification teams
2. Use the grading script (`storage/scripts/grade_research.py`) for automated quality assessment
3. Cap sub-agent count at 4-5 per team for optimal efficiency per token
4. Pre-build data schemas before dispatching research
5. Cross-reference findings between teams to catch gaps
