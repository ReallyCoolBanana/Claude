### MCP Server Evaluation
- **Alpha Vantage MCP:** Recommended. Official vendor, one-line setup, 50+ indicators, free tier (25 req/day)
- **Yahoo Finance MCP:** Free but unofficial endpoints, fragile
- **StockMCP:** More complex setup, less mature

## Lessons Learned
1. **pick_logger.py dramatically speeds output.** Creating 4 picks took minutes vs. manually writing files in Session 1.
2. **S1 picks are essentially flat after same-day validation** — too early for meaningful performance data. Session 4 will have more signal.
3. **FDA regulatory risk (NVO) was not captured by our initial sentiment analysis** — future screens should weight regulatory pipeline risk more heavily for pharma picks.
4. **Sector diversification is valuable.** Moving from 3 sectors (S1) to 7 sectors (S1+S2) reduces concentration risk.
5. **Deep value exists outside tech.** C at 10.5x forward P/E and CMCSA at 5.8x trailing P/E are extraordinary valuations for established franchises.
