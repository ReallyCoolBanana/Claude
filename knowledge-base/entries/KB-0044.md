# Foundational AI Academic Papers Catalog (Wikipedia Citation Crawl)

## Context
Crawled 12 Wikipedia articles under Category:Artificial_intelligence to extract academic paper citations with PDF links. Used 7 parallel research agents covering: Artificial Intelligence (main), Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Natural Language Processing, Neural Networks + Transformers, AI Safety + Ethics + Existential Risk, and Computer Vision + Reinforcement Learning + Robotics.

## Method
Each agent fetched the Wikipedia article (or reconstructed citations via search when Wikipedia returned 403), identified all academic papers in the references section, located PDF URLs (primarily arxiv.org), and extracted title/authors/year/summary from the actual PDFs where possible. Results were deduplicated across agents.

## Result
**79+ unique academic papers cataloged** across 11 categories spanning 1950-2025.

## Lessons Learned
- Wikipedia blocks direct scraping (403) but citations can be reconstructed via search + cross-referencing
- arXiv is the most reliable source for freely accessible AI paper PDFs
- Significant overlap exists between articles (e.g., Transformer paper cited in 5+ articles)
- Deduplication is essential when crawling multiple related articles
- Foundational papers from the 1950s-1980s are harder to find as PDFs; often hosted on university course pages

## Recommendations
- Use this catalog as a starting point for deeper research into any AI subdomain
- arXiv PDF links are the most stable and accessible
- For papers behind paywalls (Nature, Science, IEEE), check author homepages and university mirrors
- The AI Safety section is the most rapidly growing area with many 2021-2025 publications
- Cross-reference with Google Scholar citation counts for impact assessment
