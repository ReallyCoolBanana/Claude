## Lessons Learned
1. **Macro alignment matters more than pure fundamentals.** Session 5 picks were selected to align with the late-cycle rotation theme (value > growth, real assets > tech).
2. **Contrarian plays need clear catalysts.** LW (down 55% from peak) has the activist catalyst; VICI has the rate-cut catalyst. Without these, "cheap" stocks stay cheap.
3. **FRED tool is immediately useful.** Real economic data anchors analysis vs. relying on WebSearch for macro numbers.
4. **Portfolio diversification across 13 sectors** provides natural hedging but all positions are still long — a structural risk in a VIX 25+ environment.
