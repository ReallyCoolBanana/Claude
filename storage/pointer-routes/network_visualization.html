Key Insights from 58-Agent Run-006

KB = Connective Tissue

SCR = Primary Bridge
Scripts receive the most inbound edges (1,654) and serve as cross-domain bridges between all other node types.
Wiki = One-Way Fringe

Tangential Paradox
