# Stock Pick: VEEV

## Thesis
Veeva Systems is a high-quality compounder trading at a 32% discount to Morningstar's fair value estimate of ~$280 after a post-earnings selloff. The company beat Q4 estimates (EPS $2.06 vs $1.94, revenue $836M vs $811M, +16% YoY) but the stock declined, creating a disconnect between fundamentals and price. Veeva is debt-free with $5B+ in cash, has 84% subscription revenue, a wide moat in life sciences cloud/CRM, and is executing a successful Vault CRM migration. The $100M Ostro acquisition adds AI-powered healthcare engagement capabilities. With 24 Buy ratings and an average target of $280-$307, analyst consensus implies 47-64% upside.

**Moat assessment:** Wide. Dominant vertical cloud platform for life sciences with deep regulatory workflow integration. Switching costs are extremely high — pharma companies build critical processes around Veeva's platform. 84% subscription revenue provides recurring revenue visibility.

### Risk Factors
- **Valuation still premium:** 35x trailing P/E is expensive even for quality.
- **Institutional outflows:** UBS Asset Management removed 71.9% of its position in Q4 2025.
- **Salesforce competition:** Salesforce Life Sciences Cloud entry poses a long-term competitive threat.
- **Insider selling:** 7 insider sales, 0 purchases in last 6 months (routine but notable).
- **Healthcare sector exposure:** Policy/regulatory changes could impact pharma IT spending.

## Exit Criteria
- **Target:** $280 (average analyst price target, ~48% upside). Represents ~32x forward earnings.
- **Stop loss:** $160 (15.3% downside). Below recent support levels.
- **Time-based exit:** Re-evaluate after Q1 FY2027 earnings (June 3, 2026). Vault CRM migration progress is the key metric.
