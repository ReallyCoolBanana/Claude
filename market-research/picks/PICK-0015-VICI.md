# Stock Pick: VICI

## Thesis
VICI Properties is the best-positioned gaming REIT for a rate-cut environment, trading at a forward P/E of 10x with a 70.2% profit margin and triple-net lease structure that eliminates operating cost volatility. The stock is trading near the bottom of its 52-week range and below its 200-day SMA, creating an attractive entry point. Two macro catalysts: (1) expected Fed rate cuts in 2026 directly boost REIT valuations by lowering discount rates and reducing borrowing costs, and (2) VICI's experiential real estate portfolio (Caesars Palace, MGM Grand, Venetian) benefits from resilient consumer spending on experiences over goods. The 5.5%+ dividend yield with a growing payout provides downside protection while waiting for revaluation.

### Risk Factors
- **Interest rate sensitivity:** If the Fed delays rate cuts or raises rates, REIT valuations compress further. VICI is already near 52-week lows partly due to rate uncertainty.
- **Tenant concentration:** Heavy exposure to Caesars Entertainment and MGM Resorts. Tenant distress would impact rent payments despite triple-net structure.
- **Gaming sector risk:** Recession could reduce casino visitation and tenant revenue, though triple-net leases provide contractual protection.
- **Limited growth upside:** Revenue growth is modest (4%) with limited avenues for aggressive expansion beyond acquisitions.

## Exit Criteria
- **Target:** $34 (consensus average, ~17% upside + 5.5% dividend yield = ~23% total return potential).
- **Stop loss:** $24.50 (15.5% downside). Below 52-week low support.
- **Time-based exit:** Re-evaluate after Q1 2026 earnings. If AFFO guidance disappoints or tenant credit concerns emerge, reassess.
- **Rate catalyst:** If Fed signals no rate cuts in 2026, tighten stop to $27.
