# Stock Pick: EME

## Thesis
EMCOR is an execution machine trading at a reasonable 26x P/E with 20% EPS growth, a pristine balance sheet (D/E 0.13, net cash position), and a $13.25B backlog driven by the AI data center and electrical infrastructure buildout. The US Electrical segment grew 62% YoY in Q4 — a staggering rate for a construction services company. The stock pulled back 12% post-earnings despite beating estimates, creating an attractive entry point. EMCOR is the "picks and shovels" play on the AI infrastructure super-cycle with lower valuation risk than the tech names themselves.

### Risk Factors
- **CEO selling:** Anthony Guzzi sold $26.3M in stock on March 5. However, he retains a $124M position.
- **2026 margin guidance:** Operating margin guided to 9.0-9.4% vs. 10.1% in 2025. Conservative guidance or real margin pressure?
- **Cyclicality:** Construction spending is cyclical. A slowdown in data center buildout would impact growth.
- **Concentration risk:** Heavy dependence on electrical/data center segment for growth.
