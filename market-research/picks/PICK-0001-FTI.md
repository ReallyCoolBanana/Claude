# Stock Pick: FTI

## Thesis
TechnipFMC is fundamentally undervalued with a PEG ratio of ~1.0, a transformed balance sheet (net cash of $602M), and a record $16.6B backlog providing multi-year revenue visibility. The subsea services market is in a structural upcycle driven by deepwater project sanctioning, and the Iran conflict / oil price surge above $100/bbl further accelerates offshore capex decisions. Insiders are selling heavily, which is a red flag, but the fundamental case is too compelling to ignore — Moody's just upgraded to investment grade, FCF surged 113% YoY to $1.45B, and the Piotroski F-Score is a perfect 9/9.
