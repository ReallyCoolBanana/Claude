# Stock Pick: MU

## Thesis
Micron is the most undervalued major semiconductor stock, trading at a forward P/E of 12.2x versus the semiconductor sector average of 17x, despite projecting the fastest earnings growth among top memory names (50% annualized over the next two years, above Nvidia's 36%). The primary catalyst is HBM (High Bandwidth Memory): Micron's HBM3E and HBM4 capacity is completely sold out through all of 2026, with margins comfortably above DRAM averages. As the sole American HBM supplier and a key partner for Nvidia's next-generation AI accelerators, Micron occupies a structurally advantaged position in the AI infrastructure buildout. Q1 FY2026 revenue of $13.64B beat estimates by 5.9%, EPS of $4.78 beat by 21.3%, and Q2 guidance of $18.7B revenue / $8.42 EPS signals massive acceleration.

### Risk Factors
- **Memory cyclicality:** DRAM/NAND are historically boom-bust. Current cycle peak could be near if AI spending decelerates.
- **Geopolitical risk:** Middle East conflict and Taiwan Strait tensions threaten semiconductor supply chains broadly. Micron also has exposure to China trade restrictions.
- **Valuation near 52-week high ($455):** Stock has run from $61 low. Some consensus targets are below the current price, indicating many analysts have not caught up.
- **Customer concentration:** Heavy reliance on Nvidia and a few hyperscalers for HBM revenue.
- **Average analyst target ($381) below current price:** Some analysts still have stale targets from mid-2025; however, Rosenblatt at $500 and other recent upgrades suggest targets will be revised up after Q2 results.

## Exit Criteria
- **Target:** $500 (Rosenblatt Street-high target, ~20% upside). Represents ~15x forward earnings on FY2026 EPS estimates.
- **Stop loss:** $345 (17% downside). Below 50-day moving average support and round-number psychology.
- **Time-based exit:** Re-evaluate after Q2 FY2026 earnings (late March 2026). If HBM margin guidance softens or Q3 revenue guidance disappoints, reassess.
- **Cycle watch:** Monitor DRAM spot pricing weekly. If DRAM contract prices decline for two consecutive quarters, tighten stop to $390.
