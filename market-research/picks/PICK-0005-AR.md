# Stock Pick: AR

## Thesis
Antero Resources is a natural gas recovery play positioned at the intersection of two powerful catalysts: the Iran conflict driving energy prices higher, and secular demand growth for natural gas from AI data center power consumption. Earnings have surged +1,028% YoY (from $0.18 to $2.03 EPS) as natural gas prices recovered, and the $2.8B HG Energy acquisition expands production capacity to 4.1-4.5 Bcfe/d. The company is actively deleveraging (reduced debt by $300M+ in 2025) and generates $750M+ in FCF. At ~19x trailing P/E and ~5.5x EV/EBITDA, valuation is reasonable for the growth trajectory. Multiple analyst upgrades (Jefferies Strong Buy, BofA Buy $47, Benchmark Buy $44) confirm improving sentiment.

**Analyst consensus:** 11 Buy, 5 Hold, 0 Sell. Upgrades: Jefferies Strong Buy, BofA $47, Benchmark $44. Average target ~$42-$46 (8-18% upside).

**Moat assessment:** Narrow. Largest NGL producer in Appalachia with premium asset quality. Integrated midstream through Antero Midstream partnership. Natural gas is a commodity business, but Antero's NGL-rich gas mix provides margin advantage over dry gas producers.

### Risk Factors
- **Commodity price exposure:** Natural gas prices are volatile. A warm winter or demand slowdown would compress margins.
- **Low current ratio (0.55):** Below 1.0 signals tight short-term liquidity, though typical for E&P with revolving credit.
- **Elevated put option volume:** 284% increase vs average signals hedging or bearish positioning by some traders.
- **Integration risk:** $2.8B HG Energy acquisition needs successful integration.
- **Iran conflict resolution:** If conflict ends quickly, oil/gas prices could retreat, removing a near-term catalyst.

## Exit Criteria
- **Target:** $47 (BofA price target, ~21% upside).
- **Stop loss:** $33 (15.4% downside). Below HG Energy acquisition announcement price level.
- **Commodity trigger:** If Henry Hub natural gas falls below $2.50/MMBtu, reassess thesis.
- **Integration watch:** Monitor HG Energy integration progress in Q1 earnings. Any production disruptions or cost overruns would be concerning.
