# Stock Pick: NVO

## Thesis
Novo Nordisk is a deep contrarian value play. The stock has fallen 67% from its June 2024 all-time high of $142 to ~$38, bringing its P/E to 10.7x versus a 10-year median of 24.4x — a historic valuation trough. The market is overweighting near-term headwinds (negative sales guidance, GLP-1 pricing pressure, 9,000 job cuts) while underweighting the company's 61% ROE, 33% net margins, wide competitive moat in obesity/diabetes treatment, and the structural demographic tailwind of a global obesity epidemic. Medicare Part D obesity coverage expected mid-2026 could be a significant positive catalyst. This is a "buy when there's blood in the streets" setup.

**Moat assessment:** Wide. GLP-1 dominance (Ozempic, Wegovy), massive addressable market in obesity/diabetes, manufacturing scale advantages, regulatory expertise. Competition from Eli Lilly is real but the TAM is large enough for multiple winners.

### Risk Factors
- **Negative sales guidance:** 2026 guidance projects -5% to -13% adjusted sales decline at constant exchange rates.
- **Political drug pricing risk:** White House is focused on lowering pharma costs, with GLP-1s as a target.
- **Restructuring uncertainty:** 9,000 job cuts signal significant business transformation.
- **Insider selling:** Net insider selling of DKK -2.9M over 90 days.
- **Catching a falling knife:** Stock could continue lower before stabilizing.

## Exit Criteria
- **Target:** $51 (average analyst price target, ~34% upside). Represents ~15x forward earnings, still below historical median.
- **Stop loss:** $30 (21% downside). Below the psychological $30 level would signal further deterioration.
- **Catalyst-based exit:** If Medicare Part D obesity coverage is denied or significantly delayed, reassess thesis.
- **Fundamental deterioration:** If ROE drops below 40% or operating margins fall below 30%, thesis is weakened.
