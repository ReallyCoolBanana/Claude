# Stock Pick: ALL

## Thesis
Allstate is the most compelling value in the P&C insurance space, trading at a trailing P/E of 5.6x -- 36% below its 10-year average of 13.8x -- despite posting a 39.2% ROE in Q4 2025, the strongest in recent history. The macro catalyst is two-fold: (1) Fed rate cuts in 2026 reduce catastrophe reinsurance costs while boosting the value of Allstate's $65B+ investment portfolio, and (2) insurance premium rate increases implemented through 2024-2025 are now flowing through to margins. With 21 analysts revising EPS estimates upward in the last 90 days and zero revisions downward, the earnings momentum is unambiguous.

### Risk Factors
- **Catastrophe exposure:** A severe hurricane season or wildfire event could spike claims and compress margins.
- **Investment portfolio risk:** If rates rise instead of falling, bond portfolio marks could take a hit (though higher reinvestment rates would benefit long-term income).
- **Regulatory risk:** State-level rate regulation could limit future premium increases.
- **Already near 52-week high ($217):** Limited margin of safety if macro deteriorates.
