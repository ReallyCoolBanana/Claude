# Stock Pick: LW

## Thesis
Lamb Weston is a deeply contrarian consumer staples pick trading at a P/E of 16x versus its 10-year median of 24x, down 55% from its 2023 peak and recently removed from the S&P 500 -- exactly the kind of capitulation that creates opportunity. The primary catalyst is activist investor Starboard Value, which has taken a position and is pressuring management on cost restructuring. Analysts project 17%+ YoY earnings growth with a consensus price target representing 29% upside. Volume is recovering (+8% in Q2 FY2026), and the company dominates North American frozen potato processing with significant pricing power once input costs normalize. The 3.3% dividend yield provides income while the turnaround unfolds.

### Risk Factors
- **Turnaround execution risk:** Cost cuts and margin recovery are not guaranteed. If management resists Starboard's recommendations, the thesis weakens.
- **Input cost inflation:** Potato crop yields, energy costs, and transportation costs remain volatile. Tariffs could further increase input costs.
- **Competitive pressure:** McDonald's and other QSR customers have leverage to push back on pricing.
- **S&P 500 removal:** Index fund outflows are likely complete, but the stigma may weigh on institutional demand near-term.
- **Earnings still declining:** EBITDA was down $8.5M YoY in Q2 despite volume recovery. Price/mix was -8%.

## Exit Criteria
- **Target:** $56 (midpoint of analyst range, ~29% upside). Represents ~20x forward earnings, still below historical average.
- **Stop loss:** $36.50 (16.1% downside). Below 52-week low of $39.79.
- **Time-based exit:** Q3 FY2026 results on April 1, 2026 are the critical catalyst. If EBITDA margin does not stabilize or guidance is cut, exit.
- **Activist watch:** If Starboard exits or reduces position, reassess immediately.
