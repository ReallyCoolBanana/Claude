# TEAM-0028: Multi-Team Productivity Analysis

## Objective
Determine whether multi-team collaboration has actually increased productivity by analyzing all 27 prior team session logs. Deploy 7 teams using Proto A for direct communication:
- 3 Researcher Teams (RT-1, RT-2, RT-3) searching logs by era
- 3 Think Tanks (TT-1, TT-2, TT-3) analyzing researcher findings
- 1 Bug Monitor watching Proto A for issues

## Approach
1. Initialized Proto A bus with 12 dedicated channels (3 per TT-RT pair, plus global/bug channels)
2. Launched RT-1 (Teams 0001-0009), RT-2 (Teams 0015-0022), RT-3 (Teams 0023-0027) in parallel
3. Researchers published structured findings to Proto A bus channels
4. Launched TT-1, TT-2, TT-3 in parallel, each reading from their paired RT channel
5. Bug monitor ran concurrently, checking bus integrity, message compliance, DB health
6. Think tanks wrote comprehensive analysis files and published verdicts to global channel

## Think Tank Verdicts

### TT-1 (Foundation Phase, Teams 0001-0009): MIXED
- **Per-agent efficiency drops 89%** in large teams (solo: 4.0 artifacts/agent vs 27-agent: 0.44)
- **Total output does increase**: 30+ artifacts across 9 teams vs ~4 from solo
- **Coordination overhead: 15-30%** (35 orphan files, 29% unresolved items, race conditions)
- **Optimal team size**: 9 agents for research (3 leads + 2 subs each), 3 for independent tasks
- **Unique multi-team capabilities**: competitive verification, cross-team bug detection, config experiments

### TT-2 (Communication System, Teams 0015-0022): MIXED
- **Communication I/O overhead genuinely <1%** (0.3ms per message, 0.047% of runtime)
- **+16.7% data improvement is NOT statistically significant** (N=1, confounded by 13.8% duration difference)
- **20-27% failure savings is theoretical** (never empirically measured, failure injection was non-functional)
- **Benchmark bug discovery IS genuine evidence** of cross-team communication value
- **TEAM-0021 think tanks were scripted analysis**, not independent evaluation (hardcoded BENCHMARK_DATA)
- **Cannot separate Proto A effect from multi-agent coordination effect** (confounded)

### TT-3 (Scale & Maturity, Teams 0023-0027): YES
- **Qualitative capability leap**: T27 full-repo audit in 90 seconds (impossible without coordination)
- **Bug detection matured**: from incidental (T01) to systematic hunting (T24-T25: 14 bugs + 31 failure modes)
- **Knowledge compounds**: 98% template compliance, 25 KB entries, 13 SOPs
- **Accelerating capability, plateauing raw output**
- **Risk**: inward-facing gravity — teams spend more time improving coordination than doing domain work
- **Recommended team size**: 5-10 agents

## Consolidated Answer

**Has team collaboration increased productivity?**

**YES for total output, NO for per-agent efficiency, GENUINELY USEFUL for unique capabilities.**
