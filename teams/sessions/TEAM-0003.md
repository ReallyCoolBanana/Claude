# Team Session Log — 5-Team Competitive Research Operation

## Objective
Run a competitive multi-team research operation on "Quantitative Stock Analysis Techniques with AI" to test multi-agent coordination and produce comprehensive, verified research.

## Team Structure
- **Team 1** (9 agents): Broad research — 8 topic areas
- **Team 2** (5 agents): Deep research — 4 topic areas
- **Team 3** (4 agents): Verify & grade Team 1
- **Team 4** (4 agents): Verify & grade Team 2 + head-to-head comparison
- **Team 5** (5 agents): Build infrastructure, grading scripts, category schemas
- **Coordinator**: Orchestrated deployment phases and consolidation

## Execution Timeline
1. **Phase 0**: Tested Sonnet Research API — no API key available, pivoted to WebSearch-based research
2. **Phase 1**: Deployed Teams 1, 2, and 5 concurrently
   - Team 5 finished first (~2 min) — infrastructure ready
   - Team 2 finished second (~5 min) — 4 deep areas, 32 sources
   - Team 1 finished third (~6 min) — 8 broad areas, 40 sources
3. **Phase 2**: Deployed Teams 3 and 4 concurrently
   - Team 4 finished first (~4 min) — graded Team 2 at B+ (81/100)
   - Team 3 finished second (~6 min) — graded Team 1 at B+ (80/100)
4. **Phase 3**: Consolidated all results into final report

## Decisions Made
1. Pivoted from Sonnet API (no key) to WebSearch-based research — equally effective for live data
2. Ran teams in dependency-ordered phases rather than all at once
3. Used Team 5's automated grading script for objective scoring
4. Compiled results from agent transcripts when file writes didn't persist
5. Stored all findings in `market-research/methods/ai-quant-techniques/`
