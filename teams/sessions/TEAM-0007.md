### Phase 3: Archive Team Verification
Single agent verified all 10 files: 3 new tools, 2 new utilities, 5 modified methods. All passed.

## Decisions Made
1. Chose to test all 4 configs on identical topics for fair comparison
2. Selected top 3 inefficiencies by consensus (flagged by all 4 configs) for coding teams
3. Selected top 3 archive tools by priority (all 4 configs agreed on) for coding teams
4. Ran index_rebuilder --fix to auto-repair index inconsistencies found by tools
5. Used archive team to verify rather than re-run benchmarks (tools are new, no prior benchmark baseline)
