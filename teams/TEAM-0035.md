# Team Session Log: TEAM-0035 — First Market Research Sprint

## Objective
Break the zero-output streak by producing 3-5 stock picks using the established methodology guides (fundamental.md, sentiment.md) and pick template. Enforce SOP-023 anti-recursion constraints: 80% domain ratio, 0% infrastructure work.

## Approach
- **Team size:** 5 agents (SOP-022 SIZE-03 pattern)
- **Domain ratio:** 80% (4/5 agents on domain work)
- **Infrastructure work:** 0% (zero agents on infrastructure)
- **Method:** 4-phase pipeline — Intelligence, Research, Analysis, Output
- **Tools used:** WebSearch, WebFetch, kb-search MCP, Read/Write/Edit

### Phase Execution
1. **Phase 1 — Intelligence (parallel):** Two agents gathered macro backdrop (S&P 500, VIX, Fed, GDP, CPI, 10Y yield) and screened 11 S&P 500 sectors. Selected top 4: Healthcare, Industrials, Energy, Materials.
2. **Phase 2 — Research (parallel):** Fundamental analyst evaluated all 12 candidates on valuation, growth, balance sheet, profitability, and moat. Sentiment analyst evaluated analyst ratings, insider activity, short interest, news sentiment, and catalysts.
3. **Phase 3 — Scoring:** Applied composite formula (Fund 50% + Sent 30% + Macro 20%) to rank all 12 candidates. Selected top 5.
4. **Phase 4 — Output:** Wrote 5 pick files, updated index.json, created KB-0046, wrote this session log.

## Decisions Made
1. **All long positions:** Given elevated VIX and macro uncertainty, we considered shorts but opted for long-only picks with clearly defined stop losses. Rationale: first sprint should establish baseline methodology before adding complexity.
2. **Fundamental-led scoring:** Weighted fundamentals at 50% because the methodology guides emphasize intrinsic value analysis. Sentiment at 30% captures near-term positioning. Macro at 20% provides sector-level context.
3. **Included NVO despite poor sentiment (4/10):** The fundamental case was too compelling (P/E 10.7 vs 10yr median 24.4, ROE 61%) to ignore. Classified as medium confidence to reflect the sentiment headwinds.
4. **Excluded VRT despite top sentiment (8/10):** P/E of 71-76x with analyst targets below current price made this a momentum play, not a fundamental one. Inconsistent with our methodology emphasis.
5. **Excluded XOM and RIO:** Both trading above analyst consensus targets, limiting upside potential.
